<h2 id="research-interests" style="scroll-margin-top: 90px;">Current Research Interests</h2>

- **Generative Models:** Flow matching and diffusion models, with emphasis on probability-path design, transport dynamics, and scalable sampling for high-dimensional and non-Euclidean data.
- **Reinforcement Learning:** RL post-training, reasoning, and generative-model fine-tuning for controllable and reliable generation.
- **Geometric / Mathematical Machine Learning:** Geometry-aware learning and non-Euclidean generative models, including optimal transport and structure-aware modeling.
